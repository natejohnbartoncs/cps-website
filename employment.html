Experienced professionals

Law Enforcement Opportunities

These assignments are designed for applicants whose law enforcement experience has developed strong judgment, communication, situational awareness, leadership, and response skills.

Priority Hiring

Airport Security

Airport Security & TSA Coordination Specialist

Colorado Protective Services is prioritizing qualified current and former law enforcement professionals for airport-security assignments. Selected personnel may support airport safety operations while coordinating with TSA representatives, airport authorities, law enforcement agencies, and other authorized security partners.

Potential responsibilities

  • Support airport access-control and security procedures
  • Monitor assigned public and restricted areas
  • Coordinate with authorized TSA and airport personnel
  • Respond to and document security concerns
  • Assist with passenger, employee, and event-related safety

Preferred experience

  • Current or previous law enforcement experience
  • Strong situational awareness and communication skills
  • Experience working in regulated or controlled facilities
  • Ability to follow assignment-specific procedures
  • Willingness to satisfy applicable screening, credential, and training requirements

This is a Colorado Protective Services employment opportunity. Assignment availability, duties, and requirements depend on applicable contracts and authorization. Employment with CPS does not constitute employment by TSA.

Personal Protection

Protective Services Specialist

Provide discreet, attentive protection for individuals whose work, travel, public profile, or circumstances require additional security.

Potential responsibilities

  • Assess surroundings and identify potential concerns
  • Coordinate secure arrivals, departures, and movement
  • Communicate with clients, venues, and team members
  • Maintain professionalism, discretion, and readiness
Secure Transport

Secure Transportation & Escort Officer

Support the planned movement of clients, personnel, or valuable goods while maintaining awareness and clear communication.

Potential responsibilities

  • Review routes, schedules, and assignment requirements
  • Coordinate safe pickups, transfers, and drop-offs
  • Monitor conditions and communicate changes
  • Document assignment activity when required
Event Security

Event Security Team Lead

Help coordinate personnel and maintain an organized security presence at public, private, and high-profile events.

Potential responsibilities

  • Brief and position assigned team members
  • Coordinate access, crowd, and incident response procedures
  • Serve as a point of contact for event leadership
  • Complete post-assignment reporting
Schools & Organizations

Organizational Safety Specialist

Support safety planning and a professional security presence for schools, businesses, nonprofits, and community organizations.

Potential responsibilities

  • Support site-specific safety and access procedures
  • Observe facilities and report potential concerns
  • Coordinate with authorized organizational contacts
  • Assist with drills, planning, and event coverage

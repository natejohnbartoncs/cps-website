Company Leadership

Tom Dalessandri

Thomas P. Dalessandri is a respected law-enforcement leader whose career has been defined by integrity, conviction, and nobility. His experience includes diligent work as a young detective, thirteen years with the Aspen Police Department, where he served as deputy police chief and police chief, followed by eight years as the twice-elected sheriff of Garfield County. Now the founder and CEO of Colorado Protective Services, Tom provides the leadership, professional standards, and security expertise that guide the company.

His extensive background includes security operations, risk management, emergency management, special-event planning, and critical-incident response. Tom has also served as a security consultant to the Ninth Judicial District Court, on the advisory board for the Colorado Joint Terrorism Task Force, and as a member of the National Sheriffs’ Association Committee on Weapons of Mass Destruction. He expects every member of the Colorado Protective Services team to operate with vigilance, discretion, accountability, and respect.

Tom’s dedication to public service extends beyond law enforcement. Following Hurricane Katrina, he led a major relief effort in Mississippi that helped provide thousands of residents with food, supplies, emergency medical support, and rebuilding assistance. Working with organizations including the American Red Cross, FEMA, and the Salvation Army, he helped coordinate Colorado emergency-services personnel and critical resources for affected communities.

With more than thirty years of experience in law enforcement and public safety, Tom has earned a reputation for sound judgment, exceptional leadership, and an unwavering commitment to protecting others.

“I rest easy at night knowing the people under my watch are protected and well taken care of.”

Our Background

Built on professionalism and dependable service

Colorado Protective Services has provided professional security services for nearly three decades, supporting clients and assignments across the country. The company’s extensive experience in diverse communities and operating environments has created a strong understanding of the unique challenges, risks, and security requirements associated with each assignment.

CPS carefully selects its personnel through trusted referrals from business associates, law-enforcement professionals, public-safety partners, and respected members of the communities it serves. This established network allows the company to assemble dependable teams, respond to changing staffing requirements, and provide additional personnel when an assignment requires greater support. CPS is an equal opportunity employer, welcomes qualified applicants from every background, and is proud to support military veterans. Through responsible leadership, careful personnel selection, and strong professional relationships, the company continues to provide nationwide security services with an emphasis on reliability, preparedness, and trust.
